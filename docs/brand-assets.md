# Daemons.ai brand assets

The OG artwork was created with the built-in imagegen tool. The favicon is a matching, simplified native SVG; its PNG and ICO exports preserve the silhouette at small sizes.

- `assets/og-soul-v1.jpg`: 1200 × 630 social card.
- `assets/favicon-soul-v1.svg`: scalable favicon.
- `assets/favicon-soul-v1.ico`: 16, 32, and 48px fallback.
- `assets/apple-touch-icon-soul-v1.png`: 180px touch icon.

## Final image-generation prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset: premium Open Graph social card for Daemons.ai, 1200x630 landscape composition (1.905:1). Design an exceptional polished brand image, near-black #0b0d13 background, refined violet #c4a7ff and cyan #8adbd0. A luminous abstract soul spark on the right: bright compact star-like core, eight elegant tapered rays, an orbital halo of delicate glowing energy, translucent glass/plasma material with restrained cinematic bloom. Sophisticated and mysterious, an enduring guiding presence, not a robot or face. On the left, huge immaculate bold modern sans-serif headline, exactly two or three lines: "Give your agents a soul." Render "soul." in luminous lavender and other words warm white. Top-left small brand "Daemons.ai" with a small matching spark emblem. Bottom-left small but clear tagline exactly: "Purpose. Memory. Continuity." Generous margins, optical balance, extreme readability in social thumbnails, bold editorial typography, beautiful restrained visual drama. Text verbatim only these three phrases, no extra text, no UI mockup, no watermark, no busy stars, no stock circuitry. Keep all text within safe 70px inset and do not crop. This is the complete final composed OG card including typography.
