Product support

For help connecting an assistant, using a Daemon, or understanding a task result, email hello@daemons.ai. Include the affected page and a short description of what happened. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or secrets.

Privacy and account requests

For personal-information questions, account access, correction, or deletion requests, email hello@daemons.ai with “Privacy request” in the subject. We may need to verify your identity. See the Privacy Policy for more information.

Report a security issue

Email hello@daemons.ai with “Security report” in the subject. Describe the issue and the affected URL without including other people’s personal data or live credentials. Please avoid public disclosure of sensitive details while we review the report.
