Who we are

Daemons.ai is operated by Daemons LLP. This policy covers the corporate website, console, and Daemons.ai APIs. For privacy questions or requests, contact hello@daemons.ai.

Information we collect

We receive account information provided or authorized through Intergraph sign-in, including your account identifier and email address. We store the Daemon names, purposes, instructions, published versions, tasks, progress reports, results, shared memory, source references, and activity you or your authorized assistants submit. For shared workspaces, we store membership, invitations, roles, and access decisions. We also store assistant and app connection metadata, permissions, authorization records, and credentials needed to operate those connections. Hosting and infrastructure providers may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, browser details, and diagnostic logs, to deliver and protect the service.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate accounts, store and organize your work, provide people and services the access you approve, maintain task and memory history, manage permissions, prevent abuse, troubleshoot failures, and respond to support requests. When you contact us, we use your message and contact details to respond.

People, assistants, apps and websites, and external services

You can invite people to a workspace or share a Daemon with a Team. Their access follows the role you assign. People with access may be able to see existing instructions, memory, tasks, and results for that Daemon. You are responsible for choosing people and Teams who should see that information.

When you connect Toolisman, Daemons stores encrypted delegated authorization credentials and account/action permission metadata. Approved tool requests and results pass through Daemons and Toolisman to the executor during an assigned task. Tool-call arguments and returned content are not automatically retained in invocation logs or memory, but an authorized executor may include information in shared task results or explicitly saved memory. The underlying account provider and any configured intermediary process requests under their own terms. Removing access blocks further calls but cannot retract delivered data or undo external actions.

You choose which assistants may access which Daemons and which permissions they receive. A connected assistant can retrieve the instructions, tasks, memory, and results allowed by that approval. Information an assistant retrieves is processed in that assistant’s environment and may be handled by its provider under separate terms and policies.

For a participating app, you choose the Daemons it can access. The app may retrieve published instructions, task history, progress, and results for those Daemons, and may submit tasks if you allow it. The app integration does not execute those tasks. Information retrieved by a app is handled by the app and its operator under their own terms and policies. Disconnecting an assistant or app, or removing a person’s access, stops further access through that connection or role; it does not retrieve or guarantee deletion of information already seen, copied, or received by an external service. Review external providers’ data-handling practices before sharing sensitive information.

Infrastructure and disclosures

We use service providers for hosting, database storage, identity and authentication, and operational diagnostics. The current service uses GitHub Pages for the corporate site, Fly.io for the console and API, Neon for database storage, and Intergraph sign-in with Clerk-based identity infrastructure. These providers process information as needed to supply their services. We may also disclose information when required by applicable law, valid legal process, or to protect the service and people from abuse. Infrastructure may process information in locations outside your country, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Cookies and browser storage

The console and sign-in services use cookies needed for authentication and session security. The optional Light, Dark, or System appearance setting uses a non-sensitive preference cookie shared across daemons.ai subdomains and retained for up to one year. Local development previews use browser storage for that setting. You can change the appearance setting in the footer and clear cookies through your browser; blocking necessary cookies may prevent sign-in. The corporate page does not currently include advertising trackers or a third-party marketing analytics script.

Retention and security

We retain information while needed to provide the service, maintain its history, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Archiving a task or memory entry hides it from normal use or future recall but retains history; it is not permanent deletion. Revoking assistant or app access, or removing a collaborator, does not delete Daemon history or copies already received elsewhere. We use access controls and technical safeguards, including encryption of stored browser authentication credentials, but no system can be guaranteed completely secure. Do not submit information you are not authorized to share.

Your choices and requests

You can edit your Daemons, manage workspace access, and disconnect assistants, apps, and websites in the console. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or other handling of personal information. Send requests to hello@daemons.ai; we may need to verify your identity and will handle requests subject to applicable law and any required retention. We do not promise that removing data from Daemons.ai will remove copies already held by collaborators or external services.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service changes. The date at the top identifies the current revision. Questions about this policy or how information is handled can be sent to Daemons LLP at hello@daemons.ai.
