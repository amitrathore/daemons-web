WHEN YOU’RE READY

Invite a teammate.
Bring their AI into the work.

Share the Daemon and set their role. They can contribute to the project, and request separate approval for their AI connections. You don’t need to share AI accounts or credentials.

Shared access can include existing instructions, saved memory, and task history.

You stay in control

  • Separate approvals. Sharing with a person does not approve their AI. Set permissions for each connection and remove access when needed.
  • Work runs in your AI’s environment. It uses the tools and access available there, and must actively check for assignments. Approving a connection alone does not start work.
  • Save the context that matters. Full conversations are not automatically copied into memory. Useful findings need to be saved and retrieved.

A few practical details

Before you start.

Read the connection guide →
Can I use Daemons on my own?

Yes. Start with your own project and one connected assistant or agent. Add more approved AIs or invite teammates when the work calls for it.

Which AI tools can I connect?

Compatible assistants, agents, chatbots, and copilots support remote MCP connections with account login (OAuth). Support varies by tool. The connection guide explains what to look for.

Do I have to reconnect for every conversation?

No. You can reuse an approved connection across tasks and conversations while its access remains valid and the AI client retains the connection. Each AI connection needs initial approval. Access currently expires after 30 days; reconnect to renew it, or when permissions need to change.

How do teammates and their AIs join?

Invite people or share the Daemon with a Team, then set their roles. Their access can include existing instructions, saved memory, and task history. Their AI connections need separate approval; human access does not automatically grant AI access.

Can several assistants and agents work on the same Daemon?

Yes. Approved connections can retrieve shared context, claim separate assignments, and report results to the same Daemon. They must actively check for work. Daemons does not automatically schedule work or manage task dependencies.

What carries over to the next task?

Instructions, explicitly saved notes and decisions, tasks, and results stay with the Daemon. Connected AIs can retrieve relevant context. Full conversations are not automatically saved, and useful findings must be recorded.

Where does the work run?

In your connected assistant or agent’s environment, using its available tools and permissions. Daemons.ai keeps the shared context, assignments, progress, and results; it does not host the AI’s execution process.

Can my Daemon use connected services?

In the Tools tab, connect Toolisman and approve specific accounts, actions, and executor connections. Publish a tool policy to make those capabilities available during assigned tasks. Each new attempt uses the latest published tool policy; you can remove access immediately.

What information can a connected AI tool receive?

A connected tool can retrieve instructions, tasks, saved context, and results within the Daemons and permissions you approve. Daemons.ai does not guarantee deletion of information already received by an external provider. Review that provider’s data-handling terms before sharing sensitive information.

Can I remove a connection’s access?

Yes. Review and revoke access in the console. This prevents further actions through that connection; it does not shut down software running elsewhere or delete information it already received.

How do I sign in?

Use your Intergraph account to sign in. Create a Daemon for your project and follow the connection guide for your AI.
