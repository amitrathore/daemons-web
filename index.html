What to expect when you connect

  • You choose who contributes. Share a Daemon with collaborators and set their roles. Approve AI connections separately, choose their permissions, and revoke access when needed.
  • Your AI tool does the work. It runs in its own environment, using the tools and permissions available there.
  • It must actively check for tasks. Approving a connection alone does not start work or schedule it automatically.
  • Useful context must be saved and retrieved. Full conversations are not automatically copied into shared memory.

A few more details

Questions before you start?

What is a Daemon?

A Daemon is a workspace for an ongoing project or responsibility. It keeps instructions, shared memory, tasks, and results together while connected assistants and agents carry out assignments.

Which AI tools can I connect?

Compatible tools support remote MCP connections with account login (OAuth). Connection support varies by tool. The connection guide explains the requirements and setup steps.

Can I organize a long-term project here?

Yes. Keep the goal in instructions, plans and decisions in shared memory, and assignments on the task board. You and your connected assistants and agents maintain the plan. Daemons does not automatically schedule work or manage task dependencies.

Can several assistants and agents work on the same Daemon?

Yes. Authorize each connection separately. Connected assistants and agents can retrieve shared context, claim different assignments, and report their results. If a worker loses contact, review its progress before retrying.

Can I share a Daemon with people?

Yes. Invite collaborators or share with a Team, then set their roles. Access can include existing instructions, memory, and task history. Their AI connections need separate approval.

Where does the work run?

Work runs in the connected AI tool’s environment. Daemons.ai stores instructions, tasks, saved context, progress, and results; it does not host the assistant or agent process.

Does memory carry over to the next conversation?

Saved notes, decisions, and task history stay with the Daemon. Connected assistants and agents can retrieve relevant context for later work. Useful findings must be saved; a conversation’s full history is not automatically copied into memory.

How do I sign in?

Use your Intergraph account to sign in to Daemons.ai. Then create a Daemon and follow the connection guide for your AI tool.

What information can a connected AI tool receive?

A connected tool can retrieve instructions, tasks, saved context, and results within the Daemons and permissions you approve. You can revoke further access, but Daemons.ai does not guarantee deletion of information already received by an external provider. Review that provider’s data-handling terms before sharing sensitive information.

Can I remove a connection’s access?

Yes. Review and revoke access in the console. Removing access prevents further actions through that connection; it does not shut down software running elsewhere.
